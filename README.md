# My Favorite Series

## The Walking Dead

**The Walking Dead** is my all-time favorite series. It's a post-apocalyptic horror drama that kept me hooked for years.

### Why I Love It

- The character development is amazing, especially Rick Grimes
- The survival aspects make you think about what you would do
- Each season brings new challenges and threats
- The relationships between characters feel real

### Favorite Characters

1. **Daryl Dixon** - The crossbow guy who became everyone's favorite
2. **Michonne** - Badass with a katana sword
3. **Glenn Rhee** - One of the most loyal characters

### Memorable Moments

The show has so many intense scenes that I still remember:
- The first episode when Rick wakes up in the hospital
- When the group finds Alexandria
- All the battles with different groups like the Saviors

### Rating

I would give this series **9/10**. It had some slow seasons in the middle, but overall it's worth watching if you like:
- Zombies and survival stories
- Character-driven drama
- Action and suspense

> "In this life now, you kill or you die. Or you die and you kill." - The Governor

---

*Note: This series has 11 seasons, so it's quite a commitment to watch!*
